Jayma Motors started in 2008 with a simple idea: car owners in Dhaka deserve a workshop that tells them the truth. Find the real fault, explain it clearly, agree the price — then fix it properly.

That idea grew into a 60,000 sq ft multi-brand workshop beside Evercare Hospital Road, with computerized diagnosis, a dedicated paint booth and a team of certified technicians who work on everything from daily commuters to luxury cars.

As an authorized Shell Helix dealer, we use only genuine, sealed oils — and we treat every car that comes through our gate like our own.
