Who we are

Jayma Motors is a car service and repair workshop at 441-442, Evercare Hospital Road (beside Khanas/Khudos), Vatara, Dhaka, Bangladesh. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website and workshop, and how we use it.

Information we collect

We only collect what we need to serve you:

  • Contact details — name, phone number and (optional) email, when you book, send an enquiry, contact us or create an account.
  • Vehicle details — brand, model, year and registration number.
  • Service records — the work done on your vehicle, parts used and invoices.
  • Messages and reviews — what you write to us, and reviews you choose to publish.
  • Technical data — basic information your browser sends (such as device type and pages visited), used to keep the site working and secure.

How we use it

  • To confirm and manage your bookings, and call or message you about your vehicle.
  • To answer your enquiries and messages.
  • To keep your service history, so we can advise you better next time.
  • To send service reminders, only if you have an account or have agreed to them.
  • To protect the website from spam and misuse.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your information. We share it only when needed:

  • With service providers that run our website, email and hosting, under confidentiality.
  • With your insurer, only if you ask us to help with a claim.
  • When the law requires it.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies — for example to keep you logged in to your account and to protect forms. We do not use advertising cookies. The map on our site is provided by third parties (Esri, MapTiler and OpenStreetMap), which may receive your IP address when it loads.

How long we keep it

Service records are kept for as long as you are our customer, so your vehicle's history is available. Enquiries and messages are kept as long as needed to answer them and for our records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, unless we must keep it by law (for example, invoices).

Keeping it safe

Our website uses secure (HTTPS) connections, passwords are stored encrypted, and only authorized staff can see customer records.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or stop service reminders, by calling 01711-949110 or emailing jaymamotors@gmail.com. If you have an account, you can update your profile yourself.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the date at the top shows the latest version. See also our Terms & Conditions.
